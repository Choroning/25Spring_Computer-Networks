## 2. Schedule and Class Format

- **Credits (Hours):** 3 (3)
- **Intended For:** Third-year students
- **Class Times:** Monday periods 6-7, Tuesday period 4
- **Classrooms:** Science Building 2, Room 310 (Mon) and Room 324 (Tue)
- **Class Format:** Lectures based on the textbook, questions and answers on the lecture content, and student presentations. Classes are held in person.
- **Attendance:** Attendance is checked (the course is not exempt from attendance checks).

## 4. Course Policies

- Credits obtained through plagiarized assignments, reuse of assignments from other courses, or cheating on exams are revoked even after they have been granted, and disciplinary action (reprimand, suspension, expulsion, or dismissal) may follow depending on the case.
- Before submitting an assignment, students must check it for plagiarism with the plagiarism detection system (SafeAssign on Blackboard).
